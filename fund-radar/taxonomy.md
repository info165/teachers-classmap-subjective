# Fund-head taxonomy — education, skilling & allied digital-content funds

Purpose: every budget/scheme **head** where money sits for digital learning, e-content, smart
classrooms, skilling courseware and exam-prep content — so we can approach the department
**while the money is allocated but before a tender is created**.

How to read it:
- **Amounts are indicative and cited** from public analyses/norms (national unless marked WB).
  Exact per-state/per-year figures come from the state Detailed Demands for Grants (DDG) and PAB
  minutes once those portals are open. Nothing here is invented; blanks = not yet verified.
- **Fit** = how well it matches a content/video business: ★★★ = e-content/courseware (your core),
  ★★ = content that rides on hardware (demo-video angle), ★ = adjacent.
- **"Open" check** = where to look to see the head is funded but **not yet tendered** (see README).

Key per-unit norms (Samagra Shiksha, national) — these flow as grants schools/districts still have to spend:
ICT lab up to **₹6.40 lakh/school**, smart classroom **₹2.40 lakh** (max 2/school), **e-content & digital
resources recurring ₹0.38 lakh/school**, teacher tablet ₹10k–18k, composite school grant ₹25k–₹1 lakh
by enrolment. Atal Tinkering Lab up to **₹20 lakh/lab** (₹10L setup + ₹10L over 5 yrs).
Sources: idreameducation.org, samagrashiksha.in norms, aim.gov.in, news.careers360.com.
