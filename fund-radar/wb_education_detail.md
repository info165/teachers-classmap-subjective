# West Bengal — School Education funds, detailed (as of 4 Nov 2026)

Context: new state government formed 9 May 2026; first full budget presented **22 June 2026** by FM
Dr. Swapan Dasgupta. School Education = **Demand No. 15** in the state Detailed Demands for Grants.
Many 2026-27 heads are fresh → good "funded, not yet tendered" window.

All figures below are from public budget speeches/analyses/news and are **cited**. Exact object-head
splits need the primary docs (budget speech PDF + DDG + PAB minutes) — see "Primary docs" at end.
Blanks = not yet verified. Nothing invented.
